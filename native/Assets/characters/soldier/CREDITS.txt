Original GLB and PNG bytes are unchanged. The renderer restores the head mesh's lost material groups from the original
FBX polygon assignments (head-materials.json) and loads the unchanged PNGs with
the required bottom-left orientation. The original FBX is not bundled.
This is an authored textured human character, not a claimed photogrammetric scan.
